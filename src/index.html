Kyros AI

Kyros AI is an intelligent career coaching assistant that guides candidates from gap analysis to interview readiness. By analyzing user resumes against target job descriptions, it identifies skill gaps and surfaces curated video tutorials to close them. Users can chat directly with video content for deeper understanding, culminating in a full mock interview with AI-generated questions and graded feedback. Kyros transforms vague career goals into a concrete, step-by-step preparation roadmap.
