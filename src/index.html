Code, design,

I'm Kishore — a final-year IT student at SASTRA University, someone who likes building things end to end. From backend logic to the smallest interface detail, I care about the full picture, not just the part that looks good on the surface. A lot of my recent curiosity has been pulled toward agentic AI and RAG systems — teaching software to think a little more and guess a little less. It's where my focus is right now, but it sits alongside a broader love for clean architecture, thoughtful design, and code built to last. Outside of that, you'll usually find me traveling somewhere new or playing keyboard — good ways to reset between debugging sessions.
